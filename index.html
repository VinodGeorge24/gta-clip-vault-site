See how it works ↓

From source to release

Source
Start with footage the operator is allowed to acquire and edit. Record the original, creator, usage permissions and any campaign restrictions.
Edit
Prepare vertical drafts with local transcription, cut selection and captions. Check the action, dialogue, music and framing before review.
Review
Open the full draft in the local dashboard or the paired Telegram chat. Request changes and review the updated video before approving an exact release.
Release
Approve YouTube Shorts separately from TikTok. Where the TikTok sandbox is available and authorized, it can send the approved video to the account inbox. The account owner finishes the post in TikTok.

How we handle campaign clips

Each clip follows the current rules for its source, organizer, destination and account. We check what footage can be used, what edits are allowed, and what credits, disclosures, approvals and submission deadlines apply.

GTA Clip Vault is independently operated. Grand Theft Auto and its associated marks belong to their respective owners. Campaign participation and platform eligibility depend on the organizer and platform.
