Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This notice covers the GTA Clip Vault website and operator-run Windows application. The public website provides information about the app. The app edits permitted footage, coordinates reviews and manages owner-approved releases.

Information we handle

  • Website visits. The site uses static pages with no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers or cookies set by our pages. The hosting provider can process connection and request data, such as IP addresses and browser information. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Source and production records. The app can store source links, creator handles, permission records, audio and video, captions, edit decisions, exact approvals, delivery status and available post metrics. Source material may contain voice, image or account information. We use it to produce and review clips, verify rights, track releases and resolve errors.
  • Account and review records. When connected, the app keeps platform account identifiers, authorization records, and operational messages and feedback from the paired Telegram chat. Platform credentials and refresh tokens are encrypted for the local Windows user. The public site has no access to the app's credentials, database or media.
  • Messages to the operator. If you contact GTA Clip Vault, we use your message, handle and relevant clip references to respond, investigate and document the outcome.

When information leaves the device

  • Telegram review. If enabled, Telegram processes full preview videos, messages, chat and user identifiers, feedback, and approval responses in the paired chat. Telegram Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube releases. After exact-package approval, Google and YouTube receive the video, associated publishing metadata, selected account and release settings. The app can read upload status and available video statistics. Google Privacy Policy and Google account permissions.
  • TikTok inbox tests. The sandbox uses TikTok authorization for the participating account. The app uses TikTok's app-specific account ID and OAuth tokens to authorize the video.upload scope, submit an owner-approved video file, and check the returned upload ID and status. Post text can be reviewed and entered by the account owner in TikTok. You can remove app access in TikTok's connected-app settings. TikTok Privacy Policy and TikTok's upload process.
  • Local AI and optional coaching. Editing, transcription and ordinary quality checks run locally. If optional Codex coaching is enabled and the source permits it, the app can send a limited timestamped transcript excerpt and the identifiers needed to review it to OpenAI. The coaching route receives no raw source video or audio. OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • Campaign submissions. If the operator submits a clip, the organizer may receive the post link or draft, account information and performance evidence as required by its rules. The organizer's terms govern its copies, licenses, payout checks and retention. Starlet describes AI testing or training of submitted videos and an opt-out process. Review those terms before submitting. Starlet Privacy Policy and Content Rewards Creator Terms.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We may disclose relevant records if the law requires it or to respond to a rights, fraud or safety matter.

Storage, retention and security

Media and operating records are kept on the operator's device and may appear in private backups. We retain records as needed for review, delivery, rights and dispute handling, recovery and preventing duplicate releases. Campaign obligations and unresolved claims can affect when a particular file can be removed. Connected platforms and campaign operators control copies they receive under their own policies.

Your choices

Where applicable, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to its use. For a clip-related request, provide the public clip link and the relevant timestamp or issue. We may ask for information needed to verify the request. If your request concerns a platform's copy or account, use its own privacy or reporting controls. The app is intended for adult operators and does not offer accounts to children.

Contact GTA Clip Vault

For a privacy, copyright or removal request, message @gtaclipvault3 on TikTok if direct messaging is available. You can also use the reporting controls on the clip's platform. Keep private information out of public comments.
